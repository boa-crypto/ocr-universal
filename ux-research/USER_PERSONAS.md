# OCR Universal - User Personas

## Persona 1: Marie Dubois - Accounts Payable Specialist

**Demographics & Context**:
- Age: 32-45
- Occupation: Comptable fournisseurs / AP Specialist (50-500 employee company)
- Tech Proficiency: Moderate - comfortable with ERP systems
- Device: Desktop dual monitors

**Behavioral Patterns**:
- Usage: Daily, 3-6 hours/day during peak periods
- Volume: 50-200 invoices/day (300+ during closing)
- Priority: Speed + accuracy balance - zero tolerance for payment errors

**Pain Points**:
- Manual verification consumes 60-70% of workday
- Cannot see document and data simultaneously
- Lack of confidence in OCR requires full manual verification
- Error detection happens too late (after export to ERP)

**Goals**:
- Process maximum volume with zero payment errors
- Visual confirmation of extracted data against original
- Maintain audit trail for compliance

**Key Quote**:
> "I need to see the invoice and the extracted data side-by-side. If I have to switch screens, I lose my flow and make mistakes."

## Persona 2: Thomas Bernard - Legal Operations Coordinator

**Demographics & Context**:
- Age: 28-38
- Occupation: Legal Operations / Contract Administrator
- Tech Proficiency: High - uses legal tech tools
- Device: Laptop primary, external monitor at office

**Behavioral Patterns**:
- Usage: 3-4 times/week, batch processing
- Volume: 10-50 contracts per session
- Priority: Accuracy over speed, complex document handling

**Pain Points**:
- Contract clauses span multiple pages - context loss
- Variable document formats (scanned, native, Word→PDF)
- Critical clauses must be 100% accurate
- No page reference for extracted data

**Goals**:
- Extract key contract metadata for CLM database
- Build searchable contract repository
- Audit trail maintained for compliance

**Key Quote**:
> "A missed renewal date or wrong termination clause can cost millions. I need absolute confidence in extraction or I'll verify manually anyway."

## Persona 3: Isabelle Moreau - HR & Administrative Manager (SME)

**Demographics & Context**:
- Age: 35-50
- Occupation: Responsable Administratif et RH (20-100 employees)
- Tech Proficiency: Low to Moderate
- Device: Desktop or laptop, single monitor

**Behavioral Patterns**:
- Usage: Weekly or bi-weekly, irregular schedule
- Volume: 10-30 documents per session
- Priority: Simple workflow, minimal training required

**Pain Points**:
- No dedicated IT support - needs "works out of the box"
- Inconsistent document formats
- Limited time to learn complex software
- Multitasking across multiple responsibilities

**Goals**:
- Digitize employee documentation for GDPR compliance
- Reduce physical document storage
- Enable quick search by employee name/ID

**Key Quote**:
> "I don't have time to read a manual. The system needs to guide me step-by-step."

## Persona 4: David Laurent - Data Operations Specialist (Power User)

**Demographics & Context**:
- Age: 26-35
- Occupation: Data Operations Specialist (BPO, shared services)
- Tech Proficiency: Very High - keyboard shortcuts, automation
- Device: Desktop with 2-3 monitors, mechanical keyboard

**Behavioral Patterns**:
- Usage: Daily, 6-8 hours/day continuous processing
- Volume: 500-2000+ documents per day
- Priority: Maximum throughput with quality gates

**Pain Points**:
- Mouse-based workflows break rhythm
- Waiting for processing (no progress visibility)
- Inflexible batch operations
- Repetitive validation tasks

**Goals**:
- Maximize documents processed per hour
- Eliminate mouse usage completely
- Customize workflow to personal preferences

**Key Quote**:
> "Every mouse click costs me 2 seconds. Multiply by 1000 documents and I've lost 30 minutes. Everything must have a keyboard shortcut."
